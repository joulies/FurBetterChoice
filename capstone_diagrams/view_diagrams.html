Figure 7: Program Flowchart (Nutrition & Allergy)

Format: A4 Portrait | Pure Firestore Architecture | Clean Draw.io Native Cells

flowchart TD
    Start(["Start Product Evaluation"]) --> Species["Detect Pet Species (Dog/Cat) & Life Stage"]
    Species --> CheckMoist{"Moisture %
Listed?"}

    CheckMoist -- "Yes" --> CalcDM["Calculate Dry Matter (DM%)
DM% = 100 - Moisture%
Nutrient DM = (As-Fed / DM%) x 100"]
    CheckMoist -- "No" --> RawVal["Flag Missing Moisture
Use As-Fed Values & Show Note"]

    CalcDM --> Eval["Evaluate Against FEDIAF & AAFCO Tables
(Protein, Fat, Ca, P, Taurine & Ca:P Ratio)"]
    RawVal --> Eval

    Eval --> HasPets{"User Has
Registered Pets?"}

    HasPets -- "Yes" --> AllergyCheck{"Ingredient Matches
Pet Allergy?"}
    AllergyCheck -- "Yes" --> RedGlow["Trigger Red Glow Alert
'[Pet] is allergic to [Item]'"]
    AllergyCheck -- "No" --> MarkSafe["Mark Safe for Pet
Include in Smarter Alternatives"]

    HasPets -- "No" --> CalcScore["Compute Overall Rating & Eco Packaging Score"]
    RedGlow --> CalcScore
    MarkSafe --> CalcScore

    CalcScore --> Render["Render ProductAnalysisScreen UI"]
    Render --> Done(["End Evaluation"])
Description: Details the step-by-step decision logic of the nutritional assessment and allergen engine. The algorithm detects the pet's species and life stage, checks whether moisture percentage is listed to convert nutrients to a Dry Matter (DM%) basis, evaluates values against FEDIAF and AAFCO minimums, cross-checks ingredients against registered pet allergies to trigger red glow alerts, and computes the overall product and eco-packaging scores.
